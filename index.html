WISL, recorded flight evidence for mixed drone fleets

Lead developer with Inessa Wong · NUS Enterprise invite-only track · Aug 2026 - now

Different drone makers dump logs in totally different shapes (DJI, PX4, ArduPilot). We pull them into one schema, index the physics-based incidents, and a local Ollama model turns the repeats into a bulletin you can actually read before the next flight.

The useful bit, for me, is that the evidence stays tied to the actual flight. Anyway. The warning, from that airframe, in that sortie.
